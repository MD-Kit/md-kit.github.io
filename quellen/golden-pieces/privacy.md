## Part A: The app [SPIEL]

Package name on Google Play: com.mdkit.goldenpieces

### A1. Data that stays on your device only

The app stores the following data only in the app's private storage on your device:

- your game progress,
- your settings,
- your diamond balance,
- unfinished puzzles,
- your own pictures ("My Pictures", see A2).

The app does not upload this data and does not share it with anyone. We don't receive it.

Android's automatic cloud backup, for example to Google Drive, is turned off for this app. When you move to a new phone, Android can transfer the app's data directly from the old device to the new one if both devices support this. This is done by Android or by the manufacturer of your phone. We don't receive anything in the process.

**Deleting:** You can delete your own pictures in the app. If you uninstall the app or clear its data in the Android settings, all of this data is gone. We have no copy, so we cannot restore it.

**Purpose and legal basis:** This storage is needed so that the game works the way you want to use it. The legal basis is the performance of the user agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). No consent is needed for storing this data on your device, because it is strictly necessary for the game you asked for (Section 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021, TKG 2021).

**Storage period:** until you delete the data in the app or uninstall the app.

### A2. "My Pictures"

- You can use your own photo as a puzzle. You choose it with the Android photo picker.
- The app only receives the photo you choose. It has no access to the rest of your gallery. It does not need a storage permission for this.
- The app stores a square crop and a small preview image in the app's private storage. The original stays in your gallery.
- The pictures are not uploaded and not shared.
- You can delete the pictures in the app at any time. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

### A3. No account, no analytics, no crash reports

[SPIEL] has no user account. We don't build in any analytics services, crash reporting services or Firebase, and we don't run our own server. The game also works offline. The statistics Google Play shows us are described in A6.

### A4. Ads with Google AdMob

**No ads yet:** The current version of [SPIEL] shows no ads and does not ask for consent. The ads SDK is already built in, but it is not started and makes no connection. The following paragraphs and A5 describe how it will work once a version with ads is released. We will then remove this note.

**Which ads there will be**

- During a puzzle, an ad banner may appear at the bottom.
- You can choose to watch a rewarded video ("Diamonds ×2").
- There are no ads that open full-screen without you asking. The rewarded video plays full-screen, but only if you start it yourself.

**Who delivers the ads**

The ads come from Google AdMob. For this, the "Google Mobile Ads SDK" is built into the app via an open-source plugin. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google").

**Your consent**

- If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent after the tutorial.
- For this, we use Google's "User Messaging Platform" (UMP). It is a consent management platform certified by Google.
- "Consent" and "Do not consent" are equal options. You decide freely.
- You can change your choice or withdraw your consent at any time: Settings → "Privacy options". The withdrawal applies from then on. Anything that happened before remains lawful (Art. 7(3) GDPR).
- Outside these countries, the app does not show any ads.

**If you do not consent**

- The ads SDK does not start.
- You see no ads. The app makes no connection for ads.
- The rewarded video ("Diamonds ×2") is then not available.
- The game still works.

**If you consent**

The Google Mobile Ads SDK then starts. According to Google, it automatically collects the following data and transmits it to Google:

- your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location,
- interactions with the app, for example app launch, taps and videos watched,
- diagnostic data, for example launch time, hang rate and energy use,
- device identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers.

Purposes according to Google: advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. According to Google, the data is encrypted in transit. The SDK can also store and read information on your device, for example the advertising ID.

Whether the ads are personalised depends on which purposes you agree to in the dialog. Personalised ads are based on data collected earlier. Non-personalised ads are not based on your past behaviour. According to Google, identifiers are still used in that case, for example to limit how often you see the same ad and for aggregated reports.

**Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). For storing and reading information on your device: Section 165(3) TKG 2021.

**Other advertising partners**

Besides Google, other advertising partners may receive data, but only with your consent. You can see which ones in the consent dialog and at any time under "Privacy options".

**Google's role**

Google processes the data from AdMob as an independent controller. This is stated in Google's data protection terms for its advertising services. Google explains how it uses this data here:

- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

In our AdMob account, we only see aggregated figures, for example how many ads were shown and how much they earned per country. We don't see data about individual people there.

**How long Google stores data**

Google decides this. According to Google, advertising data in server logs is anonymised: after 9 months, part of the IP address is removed; after 18 months, cookie information is removed. More: https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention

**Transfers to the USA**

Google may transfer data to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. For certified companies, an adequacy decision of the European Commission applies (Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795, Art. 45 GDPR). For some transfers, Google uses standard contractual clauses.

**Controlling your advertising ID**

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android settings. According to Google, you find this under Settings → Privacy → Ads. The name may differ depending on your device. After deletion, apps only receive a string of zeros instead of the advertising ID.

### A5. The consent request (Google UMP)

- To know whether it has to ask you, and to show the dialog, the app connects to Google. Among other things, your IP address is transmitted to Google in the process.
- Your choice is stored on your device so that the app respects it the next time it starts.
- This connection only serves the consent request. The ads SDK only starts after you consent.
- **Legal basis:** Before showing ads, we must obtain valid consent and be able to prove it (Art. 6(1)(c) in conjunction with Art. 7(1) GDPR).

### A6. Google Play

- You download [SPIEL] from Google Play. Google processes data for this under its own responsibility, for example about your Google account and the download. Google's terms and privacy policy apply.
- When the app starts, Google Play automatically checks whether the app was installed from Google Play ("automatic protection"). If not, you are asked to get the app from Google Play. This check is carried out by Google Play.
- Google Play shows us statistics, for example installs and reports about crashes and freezes. Such reports only come from devices on which sharing diagnostic data with Google is allowed. They don't show us names or email addresses.
- Reviews on the Play Store are public and show the name of your Google profile. If we reply to a review, the reply is public too.

### A7. App permissions

- **INTERNET:** internet connection, for the consent dialog and, only after consent, for ads.
- **ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE:** checking whether a network connection is available.
- **com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID:** reading the Android advertising ID by the ads SDK. The SDK only starts after you consent.
- **android.permission.AD_ID:** added by the ads plugin. This is not a real Android permission and has no effect.
- **READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE:** added by the ads SDK. A "normal" permission without a prompt. It allows reading basic phone information, for example the mobile network type and software version.
- **VIBRATE:** short vibration as feedback in the game, for example when a piece snaps into place. You can turn it off in the settings under "Vibration".
- **WAKE_LOCK:** from the ads SDK (WorkManager), to briefly keep the device awake for background tasks.
- **FOREGROUND_SERVICE:** from the ads SDK (WorkManager), to run longer tasks as a "foreground service".
- **com.mdkit.goldenpieces.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION:** a technical permission used only inside the app. An Android library creates it automatically so that only the app itself receives certain internal messages.

The app does not request any permission for location, camera, contacts or storage.
